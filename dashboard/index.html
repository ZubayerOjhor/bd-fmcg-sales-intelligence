    How next month's targets are set

    1. Annual planLast year's actual by brand, plus plan growth. Brands that grew faster than the company get a higher plan.
    2. Monthly phasingLast year's monthly shape, corrected for this year's working days.
    3. Territory split40% last year's share of the same month, 40% share of the latest three closed months, 20% potential (population × purchasing power).
    4. GuardrailsNo territory below -5% or above +30% of its own last year. The excess is redistributed.
    5. SR splitEach position's share of the territory in the latest three closed months. Every level adds up exactly.
